Privacy Policy
Last updated: September 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Destiny ("we," "us," or "the Site") collects, uses, and protects information when you use this website. By using Destiny, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

We aim to collect as little personal information as possible. Depending on which features you use, the following may be collected:

  • Account information: if you use the Converse, an email address and username are stored via our database provider (Supabase) to create and manage your account. If you sign up using Discord, we require Discord to confirm the linked email has been verified before your account can be created.
  • Passkey login (optional): the Converse offers an optional passkey login as a faster alternative to signing in with Google or Discord. It is off by default. If you enable it, we store only a salted, hashed version of your passkey - never the plaintext - so we cannot see or recover it once it's generated. You can turn it off, or reset it for a new one, at any time.
  • Converse content: messages you send in the Converse are stored so they can be displayed to other users.
  • Usage data: basic technical data such as browser type, device type, and general interaction with the Site, used only to keep the Site running smoothly.
  • Local storage: some preferences may be stored locally in your browser.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as government ID numbers, financial details, or precise location data.

How We Use Information

  • To operate core features such as the Converse and account system.
  • To maintain the security, stability, and performance of the Site.
  • To respond to support requests.
  • To enforce our Terms of Service and prevent abuse, including preventing a suspended or banned account from being recreated under a different email or linked Google/Discord account.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Services

Destiny relies on third-party providers to operate certain features, including:

  • Supabase - used for account authentication and storing Converse data.
  • LuminSDK - used to source and display arcade in the Arcade section.

These providers have their own privacy policies governing how they handle data passed through their services. We encourage you to review those policies independently.

Cookies & Local Storage

The Site may use cookies, session storage, or local storage to keep you logged in, remember preferences, and support basic functionality. You can clear these at any time through your browser settings, though doing so may log you out or reset preferences.

Data Retention

Account and converse data is retained for as long as your account remains active. You can delete your account at any time directly from the Converse side settings. After you confirm deletion, your account is immediately locked and scheduled for permanent deletion 3 days later; you can cancel the deletion at any point during that window from the same lockout screen.

Once an account is permanently deleted, your profile and account data (including any passkey) are removed. Messages you previously sent to the Converse are not deleted along with your account - they remain visible to preserve conversation history for other users, but are displayed as sent by "Deleted User" rather than under your former username.

To prevent someone from deleting a suspended or banned account and immediately recreating it to evade enforcement, we retain a limited identifier associated with that account (such as an email address, or a linked Google/Discord account identifier) indefinitely, solely to block that identifier from creating a new account. No other account or message data is kept for this purpose.

Children's Privacy

Destiny is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so it can be removed.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Account deletion is available directly in the Converse side settings; for anything else, contact us using the information below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
